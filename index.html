How BhetiGO Works

Three simple steps to report or recover an item.

1. Report

Submit details of an item you lost or found using a simple form with item name, description, date, and location.

2. Search & Match

Browse or filter posted items by category and location to find a possible match.

3. Claim & Verify

Send a claim request. The admin verifies ownership before the item is handed over.
